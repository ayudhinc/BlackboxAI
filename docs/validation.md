# Validation gate

A demo or a prize does not validate the product. Before committing months after the hackathon, all four of these must be true.

- [ ] **Ten interviews** with people running computer-use or RPA agents on legacy surfaces: Copilot Studio adopters, UiPath and SAP shops, QA vendors, labs grading trajectories. Find the exact review step that costs them time or blocks deployment.
- [ ] **Thirty to fifty real recordings** from at least three outside sources, not our sandbox. Measure verdict precision and checker recall on them.
- [ ] **Three paid pilot commitments**, for meaningful money, before further build.
- [ ] **A measured outcome** on their recordings: review time saved, and violations caught that their current process missed.

If three buyers will not pay, the product is not validated, whatever the demo looked like.

## What to learn in the interviews

- Who reviews failed or suspicious agent runs today, how long it takes, and what they look at.
- Which surfaces their agents touch that have no API or DOM.
- What an auditor or risk committee has asked for, by name.
- Whether they would run agents inside a runtime we provide, or only send us recordings.
- What they pay today for observability, RPA governance or screen recording.
